Free help and support

These services are free or low cost. Services change, so check the website or call to confirm.

Family law information and dispute resolution

  • Family Relationship Advice Line: 1800 050 321. Information, referrals and family dispute resolution.

Legal Aid

Legal Aid can give free legal advice and may help with family law matters.

  • Queensland: Legal Aid Queensland, 1300 65 11 88
  • New South Wales: Legal Aid New South Wales, 1300 888 529
  • Victoria: Legal Aid Victoria, 1300 792 387
  • South Australia: Legal Aid South Australia, 1300 366 424
  • Western Australia: Legal Aid Western Australia, 1300 650 579
  • Tasmania: Legal Aid Tasmania, 1300 366 611
  • ACT: Legal Aid ACT, 1300 654 314
  • Northern Territory: Legal Aid Northern Territory, 1800 019 343

Courts and forms

  • Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia: forms, kits and self-help guides, for example the Initiating Application kit.

Child support

  • Services Australia: Child Support. Estimates, assessments and payments.

Support for dads

  • MensLine Australia: 1300 78 99 78, 24/7 counselling for men.
  • Dads in Distress (Parents Beyond Breakup): 1300 853 437, peer support for separated dads.
  • 1800RESPECT: 1800 737 732, 24/7 family and domestic violence support for anyone.
  • Men's Referral Service (No to Violence): 1300 766 491, 24/7, for men who want to change their behaviour.

Children and young people

  • If a child is in danger right now, call 000.

To report a worry about a child, call the child protection line for the state or territory where the child lives:

  • New South Wales: Child Protection Helpline, 13 21 11 (24/7)
  • Victoria: after hours, After Hours Child Protection Emergency Service, 13 12 78 (5pm to 9am weekdays, all day weekends and public holidays). Business hours: the intake line for the child's area.
  • Queensland: business hours (9am to 5pm weekdays), a Regional Intake Service. After hours: Child Safety After Hours Service Centre, 1800 177 135
  • Western Australia: Central Intake Team, 1800 273 889. After hours: Crisis Care, 1800 199 008
  • South Australia: Child Abuse Report Line, 13 14 78 (24/7)
  • Tasmania: Strong Families Safe Kids Advice and Referral Line, 1800 000 123
  • ACT: Child and Youth Protection Services, 1300 556 729 (24/7)
  • Northern Territory: Child protection reporting line, 1800 700 250 (24/7)
  • Kids Helpline: 1800 55 1800, 24/7 free counselling for young people aged 5 to 25.
