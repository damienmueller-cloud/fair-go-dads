Privacy

The helpline is not open yet. The features below will apply once it opens.

We follow the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This website has no tracking, no cookies and no forms.

Call recording and training review

Calls are recorded, and you are told this at the start of each call. Recordings and transcripts are reviewed to check accuracy and safety and to improve the service. Personal details are removed from review notes.

What we store

  • Your phone number (caller ID), so the line can recognise a returning caller.
  • If you agree, a short private note of what was covered and where you got to, so you can pick up next time.
  • Call recordings and transcripts.

We keep only what is needed. Records are stored securely and are not published.

Passphrase protection

Phones are sometimes shared. Before anything from an earlier call is mentioned, you'll be asked for a passphrase you chose. Until then the line won't confirm you have called before.

Asking to be forgotten

Say "forget me" on a call, or email us. We delete your record, recordings and transcripts, and note only that a deletion happened.
