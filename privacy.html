Privacy

The Fair Go Dads helpline is not open yet. No calls or chats are being taken, so we are not collecting any information through a phone line or chat right now. This page explains how we plan to handle information once the helpline opens. We will update it before then.

We aim to follow the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This website

This website has no advertising trackers, no cookies and no forms. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and to keep the site running. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we will list them on this page.

An automated AI service

When the helpline opens, calls and website chats will be handled by an automated AI assistant, not a person. It gives general information only, not legal advice.

To run the service we will use outside providers, who may store or process information overseas, including in the United States:

  • Twilio (phone calls)
  • ElevenLabs (the voice and chat assistant, recordings and transcripts)
  • an AI model provider in the United States (to generate the assistant's replies)

Recordings and sensitive information

Calls and chats will be recorded, and you will be told this at the start of each call or chat. Recordings and transcripts will be reviewed to check accuracy and safety and to improve the service. Personal details are removed from review notes.

What you tell us may include sensitive information, such as health or family violence matters. At the start of each call we will ask for your consent before we go on. You don't have to give your name or any identifying details.

What we will store

  • Call and chat recordings and transcripts.
  • Your phone number (caller ID), as part of the call record.
  • If you ask for a call back and agree, your first name and the number to call.

Planned features (not available at launch): recognising a returning caller, and keeping a short note of what was covered so you can pick up next time. These would only work if you agreed and chose a passphrase. Until then, everyone is treated as a new caller, and the line won't confirm you have called before.

We keep only what is needed. Records are stored securely and are not published.

How long we keep it

How long recordings, transcripts and other records are kept is to be confirmed before the helpline opens. We will set it out here.

Asking to be forgotten

Say "forget me" on a call, or email us. We will delete your record, recordings and transcripts, and note only that a deletion happened.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to see or correct your information, or make a privacy complaint. Contact: contact@fairgodads.com.au (this address is being set up). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). You can find out more about your privacy rights at oaic.gov.au.
