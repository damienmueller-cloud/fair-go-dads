Going through separation? You don't have to work it out alone.

Fair Go Dads is a free phone line that gives separating dads in Australia clear, general information about family law, so you can understand the process and focus on what matters most: your kids.

What we can help with

  • Parenting arrangements

    How decisions are made in the best interests of children, parenting plans and orders, and family dispute resolution.

  • Child support

    How it is assessed and where to go with questions.

  • Property

    How property settlement works and the time limits.

  • Court forms, step by step

    Read a form field aloud and we'll explain what it means and what kind of information goes there. The words in your documents need to be your own.

  • Support services

    Legal help, mental health, housing and family violence services near you.

We don't give legal advice or predict outcomes. When you need advice, we'll point you to Legal Aid, the Family Relationship Advice Line or a lawyer. See free services.

Please note: calls are recorded and reviewed for training. If you or anyone else is in danger, call 000.
